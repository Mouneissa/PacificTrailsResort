Activities at Pacific Trails

Hiking

Pacific Trails Resort has 5 miles of hinking trails and is adjacent to a state park. Go it alone or join one of our guided hikes.

Kayaking

Ocean kayaks are available for guest use.

Birds watching

While anytime is good time for birds watching at Pacific Trails, we offer guided birdwatchingtrips at sunrise several times a week.
