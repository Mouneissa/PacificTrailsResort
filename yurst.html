The Yurst at Pacific Trails

What is your yurst?
Our luxury yurst are permanent strutures four feet off the ground. Each yurt is fully enclosed with canvas walls, a wooden floor, and roof dome that can be opened
How are the yurt furnished?
Each yurt is furnished with with a queen-size bed with down quilt and gas-fire stove. You luxury camping experience includes electricity and a sink with hot and cold running water.Shower and restroom facilities are located in the lodge.
What should I bring?
Most guests pack comfortable walking shoes and plan to dress for chnaging weather with light layers of clothing. It's also helpful to bring a flashlight and a sense of adventure!
